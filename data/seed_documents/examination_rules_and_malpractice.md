## 1. Assessment Distribution & Weightage
Every course is evaluated based on Continuous Internal Assessment (CIA) and End Semester Examination (ESE):
* **Theory Courses:**
  * Continuous Internal Assessment (CIA): **40% weightage**
    * Mid-Term Exam 1: 15%
    * Mid-Term Exam 2: 15%
    * Continuous Assessments (Quizzes, TMA/Assignments, Case Studies): 10%
  * End Semester University Examination (ESE): **60% weightage** (3-hour descriptive & analytical test)
* **Practical Laboratory Courses:**
  * Continuous Lab Performance & Record: 50%
  * End Semester Practical Exam & Viva-Voce: 50%

## 2. Qualifying Marks
* Minimum **40% marks in ESE** (24 out of 60 marks in theory)
* Minimum **40% aggregate marks in Total (CIA + ESE)** (40 out of 100) to secure a passing grade 'C'.

## 3. Hall Ticket Issuance Rules
1. Hall tickets are issued digitally 5 working days before examinations via the student portal.
2. Eligibility criteria for hall ticket generation:
   * Minimum required attendance (>= 75% or approved condonation).
   * Clearance of all semester tuition, lab, and library dues (No Dues Certificate).
3. Physical student ID card along with printed hall ticket is mandatory for hall entry.

## 4. Examination Malpractice & Disciplinary Penalties
* **Tier 1 (Possession of unauthorized notes/slips/smartwatches/mobile phones):**
  * Immediate expulsion from the examination hall.
  * Cancellation of performance in that specific course paper.
* **Tier 2 (Copying from slips, another candidate, or digital devices):**
  * Cancellation of performance in all registered courses of the current semester.
  * Debarment from writing supplementary examinations for 1 year.
* **Tier 3 (Impersonation / Assault on invigilator / Leaking paper):**
  * Expulsion of both the candidate and impersonator from the university.
  * Formal police FIR filing and permanent debarment.

## 5. Revaluation & Retotaling Procedures
* Applications must be submitted through the university exam portal within **15 days** of result announcement.
* Revaluation Fee: **Rs. 600 per theory paper**. Retotaling Fee: **Rs. 200 per theory paper**.
