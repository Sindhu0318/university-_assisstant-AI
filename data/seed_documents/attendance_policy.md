# University Attendance Policy & Regulations
**Document ID:** DOC-ATTN-2026-02  
**Category:** Attendance Policies  
**Department:** Dean of Student Welfare & Academic Council  
**Effective Date:** 2025-07-15  
**Version:** 4.0  

---

## 1. Mandatory Attendance Requirement
1. Every registered student must secure a minimum overall attendance of **75% in aggregate** across all scheduled theory lectures, practical laboratory sessions, and tutorial classes in every semester.
2. In individual subjects, a student must not drop below **70% attendance** even if the overall aggregate satisfies 75%.

## 2. Attendance Below 75% & Condonation Rules
* **Category A (75% and above):** Eligible to sit for regular End-Semester Examinations without restriction.
* **Category B (65.0% to 74.9% - Condonation Band):** The Vice-Chancellor / Principal may condone the shortage on valid grounds:
  * **Genuine Medical Reasons:** Accompanied by a government medical officer / university hospital certificate submitted within 3 working days of resumption.
  * **University Representation:** Official participation in inter-university sports, cultural events, NCC, NSS, or recognized technical hackathons with prior HoD approval.
  * **Condonation Fee:** A non-refundable condonation fee of **Rs. 1,000 per subject** (maximum Rs. 3,000 for all subjects) must be paid before hall ticket issuance.
* **Category C (Below 65% - Detention):**
  * Students securing less than 65% aggregate attendance are **strictly detained** (ineligible to write semester-end examinations).
  * No condonation is legally permissible under university statutes for attendance below 65%.
  * The student must re-register for the entire semester in the subsequent academic year and pay repeat semester tuition fees.

## 3. Attendance Calculation & Biometric Tracking
* Attendance is recorded biometrically / via ERP at the start of each lecture (within the first 5 minutes).
* `Attendance % = (Total Lectures Attended / Total Lectures Conducted) × 100`

## 4. Warning Notices & Notifications
1. **First Warning:** Sent via SMS and student portal when attendance falls below **80% at 30 instructional days**.
2. **Second Warning:** Issued to parents/guardians at **60 instructional days** if attendance remains below **75%**.
3. **Final Shortage List:** Published on the university notice board 7 days before the commencement of practical/theory exams.
