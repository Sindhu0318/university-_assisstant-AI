## 1. Chancellor's Merit Scholarship
* **Eligibility:** Top 5% rank holders in previous semester or entrance rank < 1000 in state/national exams. Must maintain **CGPA >= 9.0** with no history of backlogs.
* **Benefit:** **50% tuition fee waiver** for the academic year.
* **Application Window:** Open from **August 1 to September 15**.
* **Documents Required:** Previous semester grade sheet, rank certificate, bank passbook.

## 2. EWS / Need-Based Financial Aid (Vidya Jyothi Scheme)
* **Eligibility:** Full-time undergraduate/postgraduate students whose annual family household income from all sources is less than **Rs. 3,00,000 (3 Lakhs per annum)**. Minimum CGPA of 7.0 required.
* **Benefit:** **Up to 75% tuition fee waiver** + subsidized hostel accommodation.
* **Application Window:** Open from **July 15 to August 31**.
* **Documents Required:** Tahsildar / Revenue Authority Income Certificate (issued within last 6 months), Ration Card / BPL card, Salary slip / ITR of parents, bonafide certificate.

## 3. Sports & Cultural Excellence Scholarship
* **Eligibility:** Medal winners or state/national level representation in AIU / Khelo India games or national youth festivals.
* **Benefit:** **100% tuition waiver** + free sports kit and university hostel accommodation.
* **Application Window:** Rolling basis throughout the year.

## 4. Government Post-Matric & SC/ST/OBC Scholarships (National Scholarship Portal)
* **Eligibility:** Students belonging to SC, ST, OBC, Minorities eligible under Government of India / State Government guidelines.
* **Application Link:** https://scholarships.gov.in (NSP Portal).
* **Nodal Officer Contact:** Dr. Ramesh Varma, Student Welfare Block Room 204 (Email: `scholarships@university.edu`, Phone: +91-891-2840-305).

## 5. Renewal Guidelines
To continue receiving institutional scholarships in subsequent academic years:
* Student must maintain minimum required CGPA (8.5 for Merit, 7.0 for Need-based).
* Minimum **80% attendance** in all registered courses.
* No disciplinary actions or exam malpractice records.
