## 1. Credit Structure & Degree Requirements
1. **Undergraduate (B.Tech / B.E.):** A student must successfully earn a minimum of **160 academic credits** over 8 semesters to be eligible for the award of the Bachelor's degree.
2. **Postgraduate (M.Tech / M.E.):** A candidate must earn a minimum of **68 credits** across 4 semesters.
3. **Master of Business Administration (MBA):** A minimum of **102 credits** across 4 semesters including mandatory summer internship (6 credits).

## 2. Grading System & CGPA Calculation
The university follows a 10-point Relative/Absolute Grading Scale:
* **O (Outstanding):** Grade Point 10 (Marks: 90-100%)
* **A+ (Excellent):** Grade Point 9 (Marks: 80-89%)
* **A (Very Good):** Grade Point 8 (Marks: 70-79%)
* **B+ (Good):** Grade Point 7 (Marks: 60-69%)
* **B (Above Average):** Grade Point 6 (Marks: 50-59%)
* **C (Pass):** Grade Point 5 (Marks: 40-49%)
* **F (Fail):** Grade Point 0 (Marks: Below 40%)
* **Ab (Absent):** Grade Point 0

**Semester Grade Point Average (SGPA):**  
`SGPA = Σ (Course Credit × Grade Point Earned) / Σ (Course Credits in Semester)`

**Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA):**  
`CGPA = Σ (Total Credits × SGPA) / Total Cumulative Credits Earned`

## 3. Promotion Rules & Year-Advancement Criteria
* **Promotion to 2nd Year (3rd Semester):** A student must earn at least **50% of the total credits** prescribed for Semesters 1 and 2 (minimum 20 out of 40 credits).
* **Promotion to 3rd Year (5th Semester):** A student must earn at least **60% of the total credits** prescribed up to Semester 4 (minimum 48 out of 80 credits) and must have cleared all Semester 1 fundamental subjects.
* **Promotion to 4th Year (7th Semester):** A student must earn at least **70% of the total credits** prescribed up to Semester 6 (minimum 84 out of 120 credits) with no backlogs in 1st year subjects.

## 4. Supplementary Examinations & Backlog Rules
1. Supplementary examinations are conducted within **30 days** following the declaration of regular semester results.
2. A student carrying an 'F' grade is permitted a maximum of **4 attempts** per course within the maximum allowable degree duration (6 years for a 4-year B.Tech).
3. Backlog examinations carry an examination fee of **Rs. 500 per subject**.

## 5. Revaluation & Scrutiny
* Students dissatisfied with their evaluation can apply for Answer Script Photocopy (Fee: Rs. 300) and Revaluation (Fee: Rs. 600 per paper) within **15 calendar days** from result notification.
* If the grade revision results in an increase of **10% or more**, the entire revaluation fee is refunded.
