## 1. Placement Eligibility Criteria
1. **Tier 1 (Super Dream Offers - CTC >= 20 LPA):**
   * Minimum **CGPA: 8.0 or above** across all semesters.
   * **Zero active backlogs** and no disciplinary history.
   * Minimum 80% attendance in Pre-Placement Training (PPT) sessions.
2. **Tier 2 (Dream Offers - CTC between 8 LPA and 20 LPA):**
   * Minimum **CGPA: 7.0 or above**.
   * Maximum **1 active backlog** allowed (must be cleared before joining date).
3. **Tier 3 (Core / IT Mass Recruitment - CTC up to 8 LPA):**
   * Minimum **CGPA: 6.0 or above** (or 60% aggregate in 10th, 12th, and B.Tech).
   * Maximum 2 active backlogs allowed.

## 2. One-Student-One-Job Policy
* Once a student secures an offer in a tier, they are eligible to apply only for companies in a strictly higher tier (e.g., Tier 3 offer holder can sit for Tier 2 and Tier 1; Tier 2 can only sit for Tier 1).
* Securing a Super Dream Offer (Tier 1) concludes placement eligibility.

## 3. Placement Registration & Process
1. Online portal registration opens in **6th Semester (March 1 to March 31)**.
2. Mandatory Pre-Placement Training Modules:
   * Data Structures, Algorithms & Coding (LeetCode/HackerRank track)
   * Quantitative Aptitude & Logical Reasoning
   * Soft Skills, Group Discussions & Mock Technical Interviews
3. Minimum attendance of **85% in all T&P training modules** is required for drive registration.
