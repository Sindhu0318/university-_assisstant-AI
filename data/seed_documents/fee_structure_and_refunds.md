# University Fee Structure, Deadlines & Refund Policies (2025-2026)
**Document ID:** DOC-FEE-2026-04  
**Category:** Fee Structure  
**Department:** Finance & Accounts Division  
**Effective Date:** 2025-06-01  
**Version:** 2.8  

---

## 1. Annual Tuition Fee Schedule
* **B.Tech (Computer Science / AI & ML / Data Science):** Rs. 1,45,000 per annum (Rs. 72,500 per semester)
* **B.Tech (ECE / EEE / Mechanical / Civil):** Rs. 1,20,000 per annum (Rs. 60,000 per semester)
* **M.Tech (All Specializations):** Rs. 85,000 per annum
* **MBA / MCA:** Rs. 1,10,000 per annum
* **Ph.D. (Full-Time):** Rs. 45,000 per annum

## 2. Ancillary & Hostel Charges
* **Hostel Accommodation (AC Double Sharing):** Rs. 85,000 per annum (includes Wi-Fi & Laundry)
* **Hostel Accommodation (Non-AC Triple Sharing):** Rs. 55,000 per annum
* **Mess & Dining Charges:** Rs. 42,000 per annum (4 meals daily, veg & non-veg options)
* **University Transport / Bus Fee:** Rs. 25,000 - Rs. 35,000 per annum (depending on distance/route)
* **Examination Fee:** Rs. 2,000 per regular semester; Rs. 500 per backlog course.

## 3. Payment Deadlines & Late Penalties
* **Odd Semester (Autumn):** Due date is **July 15th** of every academic year.
* **Even Semester (Spring):** Due date is **December 15th** of every academic year.
* **Grace Period & Late Fees:**
  * Up to 7 days after deadline: **Rs. 500 late fee**
  * 8 to 20 days after deadline: **Rs. 1,500 late fee**
  * Beyond 20 days: Student portal access locked, name removed from roll sheet, subject to Rs. 3,000 re-admission penalty.

## 4. Fee Refund Policy (UGC Compliant)
If a student withdraws admission before or after the commencement of classes:
* **15 days or more before formally notified last date of admission:** 100% refund (minus Rs. 1,000 processing fee).
* **Less than 15 days before last date:** 90% refund.
* **Up to 15 days after last date of admission:** 80% refund.
* **Between 16 and 30 days after last date:** 50% refund.
* **More than 30 days after last date:** 0% tuition refund (caution deposit refunded in full).
