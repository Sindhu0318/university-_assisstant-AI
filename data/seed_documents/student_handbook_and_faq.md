## 1. Zero Tolerance Anti-Ragging Policy
* Ragging in any form (physical, verbal, psychological, or cyber) is strictly prohibited on and off campus under Supreme Court regulations.
* Any student found guilty is subject to immediate suspension, expulsion, and criminal police FIR.
* **National Anti-Ragging Helpline:** 1800-180-5522 (Toll Free, 24x7)
* **University Anti-Ragging Squad:** +91-891-2840-999 / `antiragging@university.edu`

## 2. Central Library Regulations
* **Operating Hours:** Monday to Saturday: 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM; Sundays & Holidays: 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM (24x7 during examination weeks).
* **Book Borrowing Limits:**
  * Undergraduate students: 4 books for 14 days.
  * Postgraduate students: 6 books for 21 days.
* **Overdue Fine:** Rs. 5 per day per overdue book.
* Digital Library access to IEEE Xplore, ScienceDirect, ACM Digital Library, and SpringerLink available via campus Wi-Fi / VPN proxy.

## 3. Student Grievance Redressal Mechanism
* Internal Complaints Committee (ICC) & Student Grievance Cell meet bi-weekly.
* Grievances can be submitted through the student portal or directly to `grievances@university.edu`.
* Resolution timeframe: Maximum 7 working days.

## 4. Health Center & Emergency Support
* 24-bed campus health center with 2 full-time resident doctors and emergency ambulance.
* Medical Emergency Helpline: Ext. 108 / +91-891-2840-108.
